# NCERT Class 7 Science — Chapter 9: Heat & Temperature

---

## 1. Temperature & Measurement
- **Temperature**: Measure of the degree of hotness or coldness of an object.
- **SI Unit**: Kelvin ($K$). Common scales: Celsius ($^\circ\text{C}$) and Fahrenheit ($^\circ\text{F}$).
- **Conversion Formula**:
  $$\text{F} = \frac{9}{5}\text{C} + 32 \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \text{C} = \frac{5}{9}(\text{F} - 32)$$
- Normal human body temperature: $37^\circ\text{C} = 98.6^\circ\text{F}$.

---

## 2. Types of Thermometers
- **Clinical Thermometer**:
  - Range: $35^\circ\text{C}$ to $42^\circ\text{C}$ ($94^\circ\text{F}$ to $108^\circ\text{F}$).
  - Features a **kink / constriction** near the bulb to prevent mercury from falling back before reading.
- **Laboratory Thermometer**:
  - Range: $-10^\circ\text{C}$ to $110^\circ\text{C}$.
  - Must be read while still immersed in the substance.

---

## 3. Modes of Heat Transfer
1. **Conduction**:
   - Heat transfer through solids, from particle to particle without bulk motion.
   - Example: Metal spoon heating up in hot tea.
   - **Good conductors**: Copper, aluminium, iron.
   - **Insulators**: Wood, plastic, rubber, air, water.

2. **Convection**:
   - Heat transfer in fluids (liquids and gases) by actual movement of particles.
   - Warmer, less dense fluid rises; cooler, denser fluid sinks (convection currents).
   - Responsible for **Sea Breeze** (daytime) and **Land Breeze** (nighttime).

3. **Radiation**:
   - Heat transfer by electromagnetic waves — **does not require any medium** (travels through vacuum).
   - Example: Heat from the Sun reaching Earth.
   - **Dark / black surfaces**: Better absorbers and emitters of radiant heat.
   - **Light / white surfaces**: Better reflectors of heat.
